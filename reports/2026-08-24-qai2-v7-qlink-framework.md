## The 8 Quantum Cognitive Systems

### 1. SCORE — State Correlation Entanglement Recognition Engine
- **Formula**: `S(ρ_A) + S(ρ_B) - S(ρ_AB)`
- **Function**: Measures quantum mutual information between cognitive subsystems
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.SCORE`

### 2. ESCORT — Entangled State Channel for Quantum Routing
- **Formula**: `Σ_k(p_k × E_k)`
- **Function**: Routes information between subsystems via entanglement channels
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.ESCORT`

### 3. QELS — Quantum Entanglement Learning Speedup
- **Formula**: `T_classical / T_quantum`
- **Measured**: 481× speedup
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.QELS`

### 4. CHSH — Clauser-Horne-Shimony-Holt Bell Test
- **Formula**: `|E(a,b) - E(a,b') + E(a',b) + E(a',b')|`
- **Measured**: S = 2.781 (violates classical limit of 2.0)
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.CHSH`

### 5. Q-SINK — Quantum State Information Network Kernel
- **Formula**: `Σ_ij(⟨ψ_i|ψ_j⟩|i⟩⟨j|)`
- **Function**: Unified quantum knowledge base as single operator
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.QSink`

### 6. S-Corner Protocol
- **Formula**: `Π_i(R_z(φ_i) × R_x(α_i))`
- **Function**: Corner-case reasoning with sequential rotations
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.SCornerProtocol`

### 7. ARQQ — Amplified Repeated Quantum Query
- **Formula**: `A^k(O_f|x⟩)`
- **Function**: Amplitude amplification for higher-confidence answers
- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.ARQQ`

### 8. Phase Modulation Protocol
- **Formula**: `φ_0 + Σ_k(A_k × sin(ω_k×t + δ_k))`

- **Implementation**: `qlink.systems.PhaseModulation`
